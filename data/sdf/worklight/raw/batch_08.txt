**Submitted by:** Priya Raghunathan
**Approver chain:** Anselm Boateng → Rhoda Vinci → CPO office
**Req type:** New headcount, not backfill

## Ask
One additional frontend engineer, Conversation Surfaces, targeting senior level.

## Justification

Team is currently two frontend engineers (Tomas plus the hire from this summer, ramping) supporting a growing surface area — mobile, web, and platform console clients, three active design-system dependencies, and an experiment cadence that's picked up over the last two quarters. Tomas is the sole reviewer on essentially every PR touching the core rendering path, which is both a bus-factor risk and a bottleneck: our median PR review turnaround has crept up almost a full day since spring, not because of quality issues, just queue depth against one person.

Concretely, the open Loom container primitive dependency (RFC-0091, cross-team, three consuming teams including us) needs someone who can own our side of that migration without pulling Tomas off the renderer roadmap entirely. Similarly, we've got a backlog of known frictions — mobile display issues, a couple of longstanding layout tickets — that keep losing prioritization fights against roadmap work specifically because there's no one dedicated to closing out the friction backlog.

## What this doesn't fix
This isn't a headcount ask to accelerate the roadmap — it's to stop the bus-factor and queue-depth problems from getting worse as the surface area keeps growing. Roadmap velocity should hold roughly flat even with the new hire, once ramp time is accounted for.

## Cost
Standard senior IC comp band, no unusual asks (no relocation, no special equity).

Requesting sign-off by end of planning cycle so we can get a req live before the good candidate pool from this hiring season dries up.
