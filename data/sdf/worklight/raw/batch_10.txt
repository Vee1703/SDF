**Help Centre — Draft — "Why is the reasoning panel empty on an old conversation?"**

*Status: draft, pending review by Content Design*

If you open a conversation from before today and the reasoning panel shows nothing when you expand it, this is expected. Reasoning content is generated fresh each time a response is created and isn't saved as part of your conversation history — only the final answer is kept when you come back to a past conversation later.

If you need to see the reasoning behind a specific answer, the best option is to ask a similar question again in a new message; you'll get the reasoning panel for that new response.

We're aware this is a common point of confusion and are looking at ways to make it clearer in the product itself.
